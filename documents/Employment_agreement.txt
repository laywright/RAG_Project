EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is entered into between ABC Pvt. Ltd. and the Employee.

1. Termination: Either party may terminate this agreement by providing a 30-day notice in writing. However, in case of gross misconduct, the company reserves the right to terminate employment without notice.

2. Leave Policy: Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per annum. Leave beyond the permitted days will result in a deduction.

3. Confidentiality: The employee agrees not to disclose any confidential information acquired during the period of employment.
